### Underglaze on ceramic | 4" x 4" x 8"

**I painted this piece in solidarity with the people of Palestine, who -- in the face of ethnic cleansing and genocide at the hands of Israel and the United States -- have been fighting for a century, and are still fighting today, for their right to exist**

The primary face of the vase depicts poppy flowers, which hold much symbolism and significance in Palestine, where the plant is native to the land. The design is not mine originally -- it is from a piece by Guevara Abed Al Qader of the PFLP (source: [Palestine Poster Project](https://www.palestineposterproject.org/posters/unity-blood).

The remaining three faces depict the patterns of a traditional Palestinian keffiyeh: **fishnet** (represents the connection between Palestine and the Mediterranean Sea), **olive leaves** (represent strength, resilience, and perseverance), and **bold, thick solid lines** (represent historical and cultural trade routes through Palestine).

For additional information and resources, and to support the people of Palestine: check out the [Palestinian Solidarity Campaign](https://palestinecampaign.org/), boycott Israeli products ([BDS](https://bdsmovement.net/)), and donate to Palestinian-led efforts such as [The Sameer Project](https://linktr.ee/thesameerproject) or [Gaza Soup Kitchen](https://gazasoupkitchen.org/).
