### Underglaze on ceramic | 4" x 4" x 8"

**I painted this piece in solidarity with the people of Palestine, who have been fighting for a century, and are still fighting, for their right to exist, in the face of ethnic cleansing and genocide at the hands of Israel and the United States.**

The primary face of the vase depicts poppy flowers, which hold much symbolism and significance in Palestine, where the plant is native to the land. The design is not mine originally -- it is from a piece by Guevara Abed Al Qader of the PFLP (source : [Palestine Poster Project](https://www.palestineposterproject.org/posters/unity-blood).

The remaining three faces depict the patterns of a traditional Palestinian keffiyeh: **fishnet** (represents the connection between Palestine and the Mediterranean Sea), **olive leaves** (represents strength, resilience, and perseverance), and **bold, thick solid lines** (represents historical and cultural trade routes going through Palestine).
